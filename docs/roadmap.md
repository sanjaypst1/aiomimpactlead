# Roadmap

Indicative direction for post–v0.1.0. Not a commitment.

## Near term

- [ ] axe-core or Playwright accessibility smoke tests in CI
- [ ] Export workshop templates as downloadable ZIP from `/artifacts`
- [ ] Source deep-links from gate pages to `/evidence` anchors

## Medium term

- [ ] Optional CMS or git-based content preview for non-developer edits
- [ ] Localisation hooks for AU/UK terminology variants
- [ ] PDF export for governance decision packs (client-side)

## Long term

- [ ] Integration stubs for assurance tooling (evidence index APIs)—behind feature flags only
- [ ] Role-based view modes (Impact Lead vs Risk vs Sponsor) without implying HR data

## Out of scope

- Hosting or processing **confidential** agency data
- Claims of official Future Fund endorsement
