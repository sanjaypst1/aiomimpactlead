# Research summary

## Purpose

Summarise the public-evidence base that informs the AI Operating Model Impact Playbook without asserting unpublished institutional design.

## Themes

1. **Accountability and transparency** — Future Fund AI Transparency Statement and annual reporting set expectations for disclosure and responsible use in a high-trust context.
2. **Assurance lifecycle** — Australian National AI Assurance Framework (21 Jun 2024) and DTA pilot materials support proportionate assurance by risk and lifecycle stage.
3. **Financial services deployment** — ASIC REP 798 (29 Oct 2024) highlights governance, testing, monitoring, and third-party AI dependencies.
4. **Risk management benchmarks** — NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001 provide map–measure–manage and management-system patterns for portfolio scale.
5. **Operating-model evolution** — NBIM Strategy 25 close-out and WEF financial-services AI playbook offer comparative lessons on technology, skills, and change—not prescriptive policy.

## Gaps (explicit)

- Internal Future Fund AI program structure, committee membership, and tool stack are **not** in public sources; site content fills gaps with **proposed-illustrative** designs.
- Role-level JD for Impact Lead is derived from a **supplied brief**, not a published vacancy.
