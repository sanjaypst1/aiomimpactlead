# Limitations

## Content

- Playbook gates, meetings, and artifacts are **proposed** operating-model design, not confirmed Future Fund process.
- Use case simulator rules are **deterministic and educational**; they do not replace enterprise risk tiering or legal review.
- Worked example is **synthetic** and not based on real investments.

## Product

- No authenticated workspace, document storage, or workflow engine in v0.1.0.
- Templates in `/templates` are offline Markdown; the site does not persist workshop outputs.
- Evidence badges describe **content classification**, not legal privilege or security classification.

## Technical

- Tests validate corpus integrity and simulator rules, not full UI or accessibility automation.
- 3D/visual components may increase bundle weight on pages that mount them.

## Jurisdiction

- Primary framing is Australian public-sector and financial-services **public guidance**; local policy always prevails.

See [ASSUMPTIONS.md](../ASSUMPTIONS.md).
